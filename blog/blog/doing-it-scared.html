Doing It
Scared

by Alphonso Gibbs-McGlaston

September 8, 2026  ·  5 min read

I have so many examples for this entry. One of which was leaving my career of 12+ years to go abroad to live a completely different life. Back story, when I was growing up, the main objective for a successful future was to have a stable job with benefits. You know the house, married, kids, and dogs. The "American Dream" as they call it.

Nevertheless, my career was solid with good benefits and a fairly easy schedule. With all of that life of ease, living in America meant that I had to do more work to sustain a lifestyle worth living. Meaning, having a second job. At any rate, I had the career, the husband, no kids, and our dog unfortunately passed, so within the dream, I was still dreaming of more.

My husband and I took a trip to Rio and we enjoyed ourselves so much that we extended our time there. As we were heading back to the States, I asked him, "why do we go to a place to vacate when we can just live where we vacate?" That one question struck something within us to start thinking of doing something daring and scary. Leave our family, friends, and all we know and start a new adventure in another country.

"Why do we go to a place to vacate when we can just live where we vacate?"

The fear of starting over was real. The fear of having no job was real. The fear of leaving the comfort and stability I had built over 12+ years was real. All of those fears were real fears I faced while deciding to move. I knew I was walking away from something familiar and comfortable, and I had no idea what life would look like on the other side. But despite all of those fears, something in me still felt like I needed to take the chance.

Guess what, we did it. We scarily moved across the country to Thailand, a place neither of us has ever been and a land where we didn't know the language or the culture. We moved on faith and hope for a better life. The best part about this is we actually found it. We found peace, we healed past traumas, and we discovered more about ourselves.

"Doing things in fear helps you step out of your comfort zone."

Doing things in fear helps you step out of your comfort zone. It helps you discover parts of yourself that you would probably never would. I found that moving while scared brought a new life to my world that I didn't know existed.
