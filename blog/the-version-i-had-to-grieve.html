The Version
I Had to Grieve

by Alphonso Gibbs-McGlaston

August 3, 2026  ·  2 min read

The version I had to grieve was my entire old life. It was me in totality. The survival mode me. The version that was working from sun up till sun down. The version that overextended myself for people who never did the same. The version that stayed in situations longer than he should because he was trying to do right by others.

"The version of me that shied from being seen."

The version of me that shied from being seen. That took the blame for things that I didn't even do. The version that rarely took ownership of my fault in why my life was stagnant. The version that wanted attention but didn't want attention at the same time. That needed validation from people instead of getting it from within.

So yeah, I gave up many things and became more self-aware and less self-destructive. I say grieve because this was my life for a long time and in some areas, it was tough to let go.

"The grievance has been the best thing I have done for myself."

However, the grievance has been the best thing I have done for myself.
